# Goodwill partial-year valuation

**Decision:** On 2026-07-29, preserve fractional valuation years within the 36-month survival-curve horizon and discount the fractional remainder using the next annual discount factor.

**Context:** Flooring expected survival to a whole year discarded a material share of measured duration and made three- and ten-year leases produce the same two-year reference value.

**Why:** Keeping the existing whole-year present values and prorating only the final partial year recovers observed duration without inventing survival beyond the available curve. The sensitivity-year axis follows the same fractional rule and is capped by both the lease term and the 36-month curve horizon.

**Rejected:** Joining the separate five-year cohort curve was rejected because its cohort is not comparable with the one- and three-year curves. Extrapolating beyond 36 months was rejected because no same-cohort evidence supports it.

**Status:** Active
